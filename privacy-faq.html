Privacy should be understandable.

BBlood aims to treat personal information responsibly. The specific practices that apply depend on the features available in the deployed application and the information users choose to provide.

01

Information We May Handle

Depending on the features you use, BBlood may process information that you submit, such as your name, contact details, account information, or information related to a blood donation request.

The application may also process technical information needed to operate and protect the service, such as basic diagnostic logs. The actual categories collected should be confirmed against the deployed application.

Important: Do not submit medical records, identity documents, or other sensitive information unless the relevant feature explicitly requests it and you understand how it will be handled.
02

How Information May Be Used

Information may be used, where applicable, to:

  • Create and maintain user accounts.
  • Support blood donation requests and related communication.
  • Respond to questions and support requests.
  • Maintain service reliability, troubleshoot errors, and prevent misuse.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

BBlood should only use personal information for purposes that are explained to users and supported by a valid legal basis where required.

03

Sharing and Disclosure

Personal information should not be made public simply because someone uses BBlood. Information should only be shared when necessary for a clearly explained purpose, with appropriate permission or another applicable legal basis.

Depending on the actual deployment, service providers that host the application, store data, or deliver emails may process information to provide their services. BBlood's final policy should identify the providers and circumstances that genuinely apply.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to address security incidents, subject to applicable legal requirements.

04

Data Security and Retention

Appropriate technical and organisational safeguards should be used to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

No website or online storage system can guarantee absolute security. Access controls, secure configuration, updates, backups, and appropriate handling of credentials help reduce risk.

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for its stated purpose, applicable legal obligations, and legitimate operational needs. Actual retention periods should be documented before this policy is published.

05

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law and the data involved, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to certain processing.

To ask a question about information associated with your use of BBlood, contact the team through the Contact page. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Some information may need to be retained where the law permits or requires it. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable legal obligations and the service's actual capabilities.

06

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when BBlood's features, data practices, service providers, or legal obligations change. Material updates should be communicated through an appropriate channel.

Before publication, add the policy's effective date, the responsible organisation's legal name, and an appropriate privacy contact method.

OUR COMMITMENT

Principles that guide a responsible experience.

These are the standards BBlood should work towards when handling information and supporting its community.

Responsible handling

Personal information should be accessed only for appropriate, clearly explained purposes.

Clear communication

Users should be able to understand what a feature does and what information it requires.

Security awareness

Access controls and sound security practices should protect accounts and stored information.
